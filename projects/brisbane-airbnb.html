PROJECT 02 / TRAVEL × MARKET ANALYSIS

Brisbane Airbnb Analysis

A self-guided data project inspired by a personal connection to Brisbane—and a curiosity about how location, property type, and season shape a short-term rental market.

View GitHub project ↗Explore Tableau dashboard ↗

THE QUESTION

What shapes the Brisbane listing market?

I wanted to understand the relationship between neighbourhood, room type, pricing, and occupancy across Airbnb listings in Brisbane. Because I have friends in Australia, I could also compare some of the data’s patterns with local perspectives.

THE APPROACH

From raw listings to useful comparisons.

I loaded listing data into PostgreSQL and explored market trends with SQL. The analysis covered neighbourhood rates, room-type occupancy, seasonal demand, host and property characteristics, review patterns, and estimated revenue. Python supported the extract-transform-load workflow, while Tableau brought the findings into a visual dashboard.

  • Compared average rates and occupancy across neighbourhoods and room types.
  • Reviewed seasonal demand and monthly availability patterns.
  • Explored property amenities, reviews, and potential revenue differences.

WHAT I FOUND

Location and season add important context.

In this dataset, listing rates varied by neighbourhood. Autumn showed stronger occupancy, while prices differed across room types; hotel rooms had the highest reported average nightly price and private rooms the lowest. Seasonal pricing could be worth exploring, though the available sample limits how broadly those patterns should be applied.
