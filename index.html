A LITTLE CONTEXT

A mind for patterns.
A heart for the planet.

I’m a data professional with a Computer Science background, a hands-on IT foundation, and a growing focus on data science.

Across IT support and implementation work, I’ve helped streamline onboarding, asset management, software licensing, and reporting. At Brightline, I support enterprise rollouts and new tools—work that keeps me close to practical problems and the people technology serves.

My toolkit spans Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, Excel, and Tableau, alongside platforms like ServiceNow, Azure AD, Intune, JAMF, and PowerShell. I enjoy turning data and day-to-day processes into clearer, more useful ways of working.

Off-screen, I’m happiest exploring somewhere new, scuba diving, caring for plants, watching anime, or catching a soccer match. Conservation and the natural world matter to me, and curiosity connects it all.
